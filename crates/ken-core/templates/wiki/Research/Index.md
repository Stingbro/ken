# Research

Has a past session already answered your question? What past sessions found is here, each dated, along with the Ingestion inbox. Not here: what's true now → [[Current]]; a ruling → the decisions log.

[[#Folders]] · [[#Belongs Here]]

## Folders

| folder | holds |
|---|---|
| `Findings/` | one dated page per question a session or spike answered · written from `Templates/Finding.md` |
| `Ingestion/` | the inbox for meetings, recordings, documents and notes · [[Ingestion]] |

## Belongs Here

A Research page is evidence from the day it was written. Cite it with its date, and don't read it as the current state or as the rules. It keeps its `sources:` and nobody checks it again. Once something is checked and still holds, it moves to the page that owns it, with the finding as a source.
