# Ingestion

The inbox for material that is not yet source of truth: meeting transcripts, recordings, user test notes, documents, exports, pasted notes. It empties.

| folder | holds | rule |
|---|---|---|
| `Raw/` | what was dropped, filenames dated or sourced | an inbox that empties, not an archive that grows; not an agent research source |
| `Ingested/` | one dated note per source, the source beside it | evidence at the time; cite it, do not read it as current doctrine; not an agent research source |

## Workflow

1. Drop the source in `Raw/`. A recording made in Ken lands here as its transcript.
2. It is read and a note is drafted from `Templates/Ingested-note.md`: who was present, what was said with the quotes, what it overturns.
3. Everything the note calls for is written. Each item cites the note. Page edits go through the tool's staging. Requests go on Intake. Ideas go on Intake's Ideas tab as Wright's rows, grey until a person has seen them. Escalations go to the people named. Each "I will" becomes an item on that person's day in Ken. The source moves beside the note.
4. One card of key takeaways per source, on Ken's Review screen: what it overturns, what was written and where, and what waits. A person reads it and undoes a row that is wrong; nobody confirms a note, a link or a small correction. Only three things wait: a ruling, for its decider; a change to Ways-of-Working, Conventions or a rule, as a ticket; and an edit the tool holds in staging because it rewrites more than a fifth of a page or lands on one a person changed during the run.
5. What still matters in six months becomes a real page in its section, with the note as a source. What is now true about the project or the team is rewritten on its page in [[Current]], never appended to. A note is promoted or linked, never copied.

The raw record is never edited down. A copy shorter than its source carries an ABRIDGED banner naming the full record.
