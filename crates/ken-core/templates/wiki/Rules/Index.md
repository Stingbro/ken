# Rules

What does the team hold itself to? Each rule gets one page here, with the incident behind it. Rules are binding, so a review reads a change against them. Not here: a ruling, in the words it was made in → `Decisions/DECISIONS.md`; a rule a linter already enforces → the linter's config.

## Belongs Here

You write a rule from `Templates/Rule.md`, and its short form goes in `CLAUDE.md` too. When the two disagree, `CLAUDE.md` wins and the page gets fixed the same day. A repo with a component library keeps its rules on `Rules/Component-Library.md`, from `Templates/Component-Library.md`.
