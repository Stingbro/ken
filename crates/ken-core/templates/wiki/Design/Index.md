# Design

## Belongs Here

You write a design note from `Templates/Design-Note.md`. It says what problem it answers, what was chosen, what was chosen against and why, and which rulings it rests on. It cites rulings by their D-nnn and never copies them, because the decisions log is the record and a copy drifts from it.
