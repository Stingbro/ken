## What Belongs Here

Anything that constrains **how** work is done, regardless of what the work is.
A note belongs here if breaking it would be a process failure rather than a
wrong answer.

Every rule is one note, so **backlinks show which work invoked which rule**.

## What Does Not

Facts about the system (→ {{Domain}}), what we chose to build (→ Design), what
a session found (→ Research), who holds which role and area now (→ Current).
